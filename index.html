What's Included

This half-day workplace resilience training addresses psychosocial risk with two distinct components:

  • PRisMA Stage I screen (LEO26): Complete the 26-item psychosocial risk screening tool (Job Control, Work Demand, Job Support) at the start of the session
  • PR6 Composure & Collaboration: Learn practical resilience skills for maintaining composure under workplace stress and developing collaboration skills for effective teamwork in challenging environments

The LEO26 screen is bundled into this course for HRD Corp levy convenience. Screening and skills training are separate components within the half-day.

Why psychosocial risk screening matters

  • Malaysian employers have a duty under OSHA 1994 (section 18B) to assess workplace safety and health risks and put controls in place where needed.
  • DOSH PRisMA 2024 guides employers on screening psychosocial risks and then managing them in a structured way.
  • Stage I (LEO26) screens how work design and support relate to psychosocial risk (Job Control, Work Demand, Job Support).
  • Screening is the first step — it does not replace later assessment, control actions, or reassessment where risk is High.
  • Appointing a Psychosocial Trained Person (PTP) helps employers run PRisMA properly and keep confidential records.
  • This half-day course includes a LEO26 screen for HRD levy convenience, plus PR6 workplace resilience skills — it is not a full organisational PRisMA cycle.

Need the full PRisMA cycle beyond a Stage I screen? ECS supports employers with PTP-led psychosocial risk assessment under the DOSH 2024 Guidelines — Stage I (LEO26), Stage II (EPC23 where organisational risk is High), and Stage III (PRiMA actions, reassessment, and records). This is a compliance engagement, separate from our HRD Corp claimable Workplace Resilience half-day.

Full PRisMA Cycle Stages

  • Stage I — LEO26 screen for the work unit: Psychosocial risk screening covering Job Control, Work Demand, and Job Support
  • Stage II — EPC23 practice check when any organisational High: Deeper assessment of workplace practices when high-risk findings emerge from Stage I
  • Stage III — PRiMA plan of actions + reassessment schedule + recordkeeping: Development and implementation of risk control measures, ongoing monitoring, and maintaining confidential records
  • Led by a Psychosocial Trained Person (PTP): Ensures proper PRisMA methodology and regulatory alignment

The employer owns the duty under OSHA section 18B; the PTP assists in conducting the assessment properly. This full-cycle engagement addresses organisational psychosocial risk management, not training delivery.

Executive Programme Earned Leadership™

HRD Corp Claimable

Earned Leadership™ — 14-Hour Executive Programme · Decision · Communication · Aura (earned trust / presence)

01

Philosophy

Earned Leadership™ is influence you build through judgment and trust — not authority that comes with a title or rank. Leadership is judgment exercised before the outcome is known, so it is taught from inside uncertainty, not from hindsight success stories.

02

Application

Participants practise three pillars — Decision (decision rights under uncertainty and change), Communication (including hard conversations that protect dignity), and Aura (executive presence / gravitas as earned trust — not charisma or projection) — using the Practice Loop on cases and their own live leadership challenge.

03

Impact

Leaders leave able to allocate decision rights more clearly, hold harder conversations with dignity, and build earned trust over time — with observable self-marks on the Earned Ladder and a written 90-day plan (one commitment per pillar).
