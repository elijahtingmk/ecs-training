Workplace Resilience for Psychosocial Risk: Composure and Collaboration

Format: Half-day / 4 hours
Includes: LEO26 psychosocial risk screen (no separate fee)

What's Included

This half-day workplace resilience training addresses psychosocial risk with two distinct components:

  • PRisMA Stage I LEO26 Screen: Complete the 26-item psychosocial risk screening tool (Job Control, Work Demand, Job Support) at the start of the session
  • PR6 Composure & Collaboration: Learn practical resilience skills for maintaining composure under workplace stress and developing collaboration skills for effective teamwork in challenging environments

The LEO26 screen is bundled into this course for HRD Corp levy convenience. Screening and skills training are separate components within the half-day.

Who Should Attend

  • Employees in work units undergoing psychosocial risk (LEO26) screening
  • Staff required to complete LEO26 psychosocial risk screening
  • HR / L&D supporting workplace psychosocial risk assessment (PRisMA)
  • Employers addressing psychosocial risk factors in their organizations
  • Anyone seeking practical composure and collaboration skills for challenging work environments

Earned Leadership™

Transform your organizational culture with a leadership philosophy rooted in trust, respect, and mutual commitment.

Philosophy

Earned Leadership™ recognizes that true authority comes not from position, but from the trust and respect leaders earn through their actions and character.

Application

Build high-performing teams where leadership is a reciprocal relationship, not a one-way directive. Create environments where people choose to follow because they believe in the leader's vision and integrity.

Impact

Organizations that embrace Earned Leadership™ experience higher engagement, stronger collaboration, and more sustainable results as team members become invested partners in shared success.
